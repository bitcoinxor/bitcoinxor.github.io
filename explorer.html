How to read this page: newest block on the right, history running off the left edge — scroll the row to go back in time. On each block, the bar is how full it is, the fee number is the median rate its transactions paid, and the orange text is the miner's coinbase tag. The dashed outline is the next block, assembling live from the mempool; when it's mined it slides into the row. Click any block for its full header, byte by byte, and its transactions.
